      Browser Assistant Playbook

      Copy-paste these into the Claude side panel in Chrome. Each ▸ button copies the command.

      1 · Pull leads out of Ringy (weekly)

      Log into Ringy, go to the Leads page, then:

      Click the Generate CSV button, check "Include all custom fields", and download the CSV file of my leads.

      Then come back here → Import CSV → choose "Update it" for duplicates.

      2 · Grab client notes from the USHA portal

      Log in yourself and open one client's page, then:

      Copy all the notes and client details visible on this page as plain text so I can paste them into my CRM.

      Paste the result into that lead's "Add a note" box here. One client at a time — don't bulk-automate the carrier portal.

      3 · Confirm today's appointments

      Check your "Today's appointments" panel here, then in Ringy (or your texting tool):

      Draft a short, friendly appointment-confirmation text for [NAME] confirming our call at [TIME] today. Keep it under 30 words and don't send it — show it to me first.

      Tip: let the assistant open this app

      Claude in Chrome may not work on file:// pages by default. In Chrome → Extensions → Claude → Details → turn on "Allow access to file URLs" if you want it to type directly into this app.
